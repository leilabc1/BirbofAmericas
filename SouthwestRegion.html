Greatee Roadrunner

  • Located in: Primarily found in arid, open, and semi-arid regions of the southwestern and western United States as well as Mexico.
  • The greater roadrunner is identified by their spikey black head feathers, long black tail feathers, and long slender legs.
  • - Diet: The greater roadrunner is a voracious, opportunistic omnivore whose diet consists of insects and arthropods, reptiles, amphibians, small mammals, and birds.
