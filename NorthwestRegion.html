Cassins Finch
Haemorhous Cassinii

  • Located in: these finches live across the mountains of western North America, ranging from southern British Columbia down through the western United States and into northern New Mexico.
  • Cassin's finches are known for their distinct gender differences as males have red heads with brown bodies that are cream and streaked with dark brown stripes and females are brown/cream with dark brown streaks covering their bodies.
  • It eats: Their diet mainly consists of seeds, buds, berries, small fruits, insects, and minerals.

Red Crossbill
Loxia Curvirostra

  • Located in: In the United States, they inhabit western mountain ranges including the Sierra Nevada of California and the Rocky Mountains and extend south through the high Appalachians to Georgia.
  • Adult males are a striking brick red or vermilion with darker brown/black wings and tail. Females are dull yellowish or olive-green with grayish-brown wings and tail. Juveniles are heavily streaked.
  • Diet: their main diet happens to be conifer seeds that they have specific mandibles that they use to pry open.

Chipping Sparrow
Spizella Passerina

  • Located in: Chipping sparrows live across most of North America, breeding from Canada down to the southern United States and Mexico, and wintering in the southern U.S. and Central America.
  • The chipping sparrow can be identified by their small, slender bodies measuring 4.7 to 5.9 inches long with a distinct black line running through its eye. They also have a brown chestnut spot on their heads.
  • Diet: Chipping sparrows are primarily seedeaters that switch to a protein-rich diet of insects during the breeding season.

Western Bluebird
Sialia Mexicana

  • Located in: The Western Bluebird lives in open and semi-open habitats across western North America, ranging from British Columbia down to Mexico.
  • The western bluebird has a striking blue body with a bright red orange chest and back. Their feathers are also iridescent.
  • Diet: They eat mostly insects in the spring and summer. In autumn and winter, they switch to eating mistletoe, juniper, and other shrub or tree berries!

Anna's Hummingbird
Calypte Anna

  • Located in: Found year-round along the Pacific slope from British Columbia down through Washington, Oregon, California, and into southwestern Arizona and northern Mexico.
  • Features a bronze-green back and greyish chest and belly. Adult males have a striking, iridescent rose-pink or magenta crown and throat called a gorget that flashes brilliantly in the light. Females and immatures have metallic green upperparts, grey underparts, and occasional pink spots on the throat.
  • Diet: Feeds primarily on flower nectar, tree sap, and a high proportion of tiny insects.
