Birds of America

        Welcome to Birds of America! The United States can be divided into 5 Regions: Northeast, Northwest, Central, Southeast and Southwest. Within those regions there are so many different kinds of birds. We can't wait to teach you about these beautiful creatures and after, we hope that you love birds as much as we do!
To begin learning, select a region from a button below the map or using the drop down menu above to see 5 different species from the selected region!
